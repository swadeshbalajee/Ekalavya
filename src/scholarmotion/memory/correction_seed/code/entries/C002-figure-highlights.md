title: Highlight boxes placed on the wrong part of a paper figure
triggers: paper_figure, highlight_targets, figure
seen: 5
---
Every model, given only the picture, put highlight boxes on the legend or on empty space. When the
scene lists MEASURED HIGHLIGHTS, use exactly those figure_region/figure_point calls, show one at a
time while the voice names that part, and FadeOut the previous highlight in the same play. Never
add a box of your own on the figure.
